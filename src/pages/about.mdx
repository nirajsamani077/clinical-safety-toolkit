## Who made it

Dr Niraj Samani, a GP and Staffordshire Training Hub's AI and Digital Health Ambassador, trained as a Clinical Safety Officer. It's written for practice managers, partners and practice teams, to make the NHS clinical safety and data protection standards easier to understand and act on.

## How it's kept current

- Each page shows the date its sources were last checked.
- The whole site is reviewed every three months, and sooner when national guidance changes. See [what's changing](/whats-changing).
- Where a page gives our view rather than published guidance, it says so.

## Spotted a mistake?

Email [niraj.samani@gpfirst.net](mailto:niraj.samani@gpfirst.net?subject=Clinical%20Safety%20Toolkit%20feedback) with the page and what's wrong. Corrections are made as soon as they're checked, and listed below.
