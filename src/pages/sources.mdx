## Our approach

- **Written for this toolkit.** The explanations, diagrams, worked examples and templates are original. Oakfield Surgery is fictional.
- **We link rather than copy.** Where another organisation has published guidance, a template or a video, we link to the original and credit it. We don't host copies of other organisations' documents.
- **Public sector information.** Some national guidance from NHS England and GOV.UK is published under the [Open Government Licence](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). We summarise and link to it with attribution.
- **Facts from published reports.** The real incidents are short summaries of facts reported in the sources listed, each linked on its card.
- **No endorsements.** Staffordshire Training Hub doesn't endorse any supplier, product or provider. Products are named only where a published source names them.

If you think we've used your work without proper credit, please email niraj.samani@gpfirst.net and we'll put it right.
