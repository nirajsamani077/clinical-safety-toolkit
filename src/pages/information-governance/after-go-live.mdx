## Data breaches

Patients' information lost, changed, or seen by someone who shouldn't see it. For example:

<div class="tiles tight">
  <div class="tile c-red"><span class="ib"><Icon name="doc" /></span><strong>Filed to the wrong patient</strong><span>A request or note in the wrong record.</span></div>
  <div class="tile c-red"><span class="ib"><Icon name="chat" /></span><strong>Sent to the wrong person</strong><span>A message to the wrong number or email.</span></div>
  <div class="tile c-red"><span class="ib"><Icon name="phone" /></span><strong>Pasted into personal AI</strong><span>Patient details put into a free chatbot.</span></div>
</div>

### The first 72 hours

<BreachClock />

Report through the incident tool in the DSPT, which notifies the ICO.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup> If the tool caused or nearly caused harm, tell your CSO too.

## Keeping records

Copy what matters into the medical record, agree with the supplier how long the tool keeps its copy, and follow NHS England's Records Management Code of Practice.<sup><a href="#ref-2">2</a></sup>

## When to review the DPIA

<ul class="chips warm">
  <li><Icon name="refresh" /><span><strong>A new use:</strong> a new way of working, or new patients</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="link" /><span><strong>Supplier changes:</strong> where data goes, or a new subcontractor</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="alert" /><span><strong>A breach</strong> that shows a risk you hadn't spotted</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="calendar" /><span><strong>A year on,</strong> even if nothing else has changed</span></li>
</ul>

## Ending a contract

<ol class="path four">
  <li style="--c:#057092"><strong>Get your data back</strong><span>In a format you can use</span></li>
  <li style="--c:#920570"><strong>Confirm deletion</strong><span>In writing from the supplier</span></li>
  <li style="--c:#0b4a6e"><strong>Remove access</strong><span>Staff logins and links to your clinical system</span></li>
