## What CQC has said

CQC's GP mythbuster on AI asks for evidence that AI is chosen carefully, used safely and kept under review, not for any one document.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>

<div class="qband">
  <blockquote>There should be a responsible CSO and digital lead for AI technologies and related clinical governance.<cite>CQC GP mythbuster 109</cite></blockquote>
</div>
