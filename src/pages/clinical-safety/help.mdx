## Common questions

<div class="faq">
<details>
<summary>Does every practice need its own CSO?</summary>

No. One CSO can cover several practices. Their job is to review and sign off; the practice team can do much of the groundwork.<sup><a href="#ref-3">3</a></sup>
</details>

<details>
<summary>If the ICB bought the tool, haven't they done the clinical safety?</summary>

Partly. DCB0160 is about how the tool is used in your practice, which only you can assess. Ask what they've done and build on it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can we use the practice down the road's safety case?</summary>

As a starting point, not as your own. Your workflows and controls will differ.<sup><a href="#ref-3">3</a></sup>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Isn't the DPIA enough?</summary>

No. The DPIA covers data protection. Clinical safety is a separate requirement.
</details>
</div>
