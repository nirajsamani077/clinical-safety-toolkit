## The safety loop

Every incident or near miss is a chance to check the hazard log. Listed already? A control may have failed. Not listed? You've found a new hazard.

<FeedbackLoop />

Keep one [incident log](/templates/incident-log) for all your digital tools, alongside your significant event process.

## Who to tell

<ol class="ladder">
  <li style="--c:#057092"><span class="dot"><Icon name="shield" /></span><span class="when">Always</span><span class="who">Your CSO, and record it locally</span></li>
  <li style="--c:#0b4a6e"><span class="dot"><Icon name="server" /></span><span class="when">The tool contributed</span><span class="who">The supplier, who should have an escalation route for safety issues</span></li>

  <li style="--c:#8a5f00"><span class="dot"><Icon name="alert" /></span><span class="when">It's a medical device</span><span class="who">The <a href="https://yellowcard.mhra.gov.uk/">MHRA Yellow Card scheme</a></span></li>

## Keeping watch

Five minutes on digital tools at the monthly clinical governance meeting, where incidents and what was learned are shared, and a monthly check of ten random examples. That audit makes a good QI project for a GP registrar or FY2.

<div class="boards">
  <div class="board" style="--c:#920570">
    <h3>Example check: AI scribe notes</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Was the patient asked, and the answer recorded? <em>Every note</em></li>
      <li>Does the note include anything that wasn't said? <em>Nothing</em></li>
      <li>Are the plan and safety-netting recorded? <em>Yes</em></li>
    </ul>

</div>

## When to look again

<ul class="chips warm">
  <li><Icon name="refresh" />A significant update or new feature</li>
  <li><Icon name="users" />A new way of using it</li>
  <li><Icon name="alert" />An incident that shows a new hazard</li>
  <li><Icon name="book" />New national guidance</li>
  <li><Icon name="calendar" />A year since the last review</li>
</ul>

Switching a tool off has risks too: finish open work, get the supplier's data handling in writing, and update the register.
