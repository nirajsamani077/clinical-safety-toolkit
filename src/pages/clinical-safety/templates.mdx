## The templates

Seven documents, written for this toolkit. The DPIA template is on the [DPIA page](/information-governance/dpia#a-template-to-start-from), and all eight are together on the [templates page](/templates). Pick one to read it here, open it in its own tab, or download it as Word or Excel to fill in. Each has a blank version and one filled in for Oakfield Surgery: use the switch at the top of the document.

<div class="note">
<strong>These are example drafts.</strong> Your documents need to be reviewed by a Clinical Safety Officer. NHS England also has draft versions of several documents available. Adapt them to your practice, and make sure the work is your own, based on your local operating procedures and team protocols.

## Supplier templates

Your supplier may also provide a DCB0160 starter template for their product, in addition to their DCB0129 documents. Use it as a starting point, and make sure the final version describes your practice, not just the product.

Templates for data protection impact assessments are in the [information governance section](/information-governance).
