## What it is

NHS England describes DTAC as an assessment framework for health and care providers to use when buying or deploying digital products. The supplier completes the form and you use it as part of your checks.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>

A shorter, updated form was published in March 2026, with about a quarter fewer questions and less overlap with other NHS checks such as the DSPT.<sup><a href="#ref-2">2</a></sup>

## Worth knowing

<ul class="ticks">
  <li><strong>It isn't a certificate</strong><span>DTAC sits alongside other approvals and doesn't replace them. NHS England doesn't endorse third-party DTAC certification schemes.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup></span></li>
  <li><strong>It doesn't replace your own work</strong><span>You still need your own clinical safety assessment and DPIA. DTAC answers help you write them.</span></li>
  <li><strong>Check the date</strong><span>Ask for the current version. A form from years ago may not reflect the product you're buying.</span></li>
  <li><strong>ICB-bought tools</strong><span>If your ICB has bought the tool, ask whether it already holds the supplier's DTAC.</span></li>
</ul>
