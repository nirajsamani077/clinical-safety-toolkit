## What it is

NHS England's self-certified registry of suppliers of ambient voice technology, often called AI scribes. Suppliers on it have provided evidence against NHS England's criteria, and NHS organisations can view that evidence through the national procurement hub.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup> The first list, in January 2026, named 19 suppliers.<sup><a href="#ref-2">2</a></sup>

NHS England is clear about who decides: "All assurance and decision-making sits with your local NHS organisation, not NHS England."<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>
