## What it is

An online self-assessment against the National Data Guardian's data security standards. Organisations with access to NHS patient data and systems must complete it.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup> The 2025-26 toolkit for GP practices had 45 evidence items, with a deadline of 30 June 2026.<sup><a href="#ref-2">2</a></sup>

Large NHS organisations are moving to a version based on the Cyber Assessment Framework. Other organisations stay on the current version until NHS England tells them it's their turn.<sup><a href="#ref-3">3</a></sup>

## Where a new tool comes in

<ul class="rows">
  <li><Icon name="server" /><strong>Asset register</strong><span>Add the tool to your list of information assets.</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="link" /><strong>Data flows</strong><span>Record what data goes to the supplier and where.</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="doc" /><strong>DPIA</strong><span>Keep the DPIA and data processing agreement as evidence. <a href="/information-governance/dpia">The DPIA</a>.</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="book" /><strong>Training</strong><span>Staff who use it are trained, and it's recorded.</span></li>
  <li><Icon name="alert" /><strong>Incidents</strong><span>Data breaches involving the tool are reported through the DSPT. <a href="/information-governance/after-go-live#data-breaches">Data breaches</a>.</span></li>
</ul>

Many practices get help from their ICB or DPO service, which often provides template evidence. Ask yours before you start.
