## How they fit together

<DcbLanes />

You can't do good DCB0160 work without the supplier's DCB0129 documents. They tell you what the supplier has already thought about, and what they expect you to manage locally.

## What to ask the supplier

<ul class="askq">
  <li>Can you send your clinical safety case report and hazard log?</li>
  <li>Who is your Clinical Safety Officer?</li>
  <li>Which risks do you expect us to manage locally?</li>
  <li>How will you tell us about safety issues and updates?</li>
</ul>

For the whole process, with templates and a worked example, see [clinical safety](/clinical-safety/process).
