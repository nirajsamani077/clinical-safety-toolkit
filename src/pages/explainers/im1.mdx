## What it is

IM1 (short for Interface Mechanism 1) is a set of standard connections into the main GP clinical systems. A tool that is "paired" through IM1 can read patient information, extract data in bulk, or enter data into the record.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>

Before a pairing goes live, the supplier has to show evidence that the connection works as it should, and this is assured nationally.<sup><a href="#ref-1">1</a></sup>

## What national assurance doesn't cover

IM1 assurance is about the connection. It doesn't look at your practice's workflows, your staff, or how a tool's output is checked before it's saved. That's what your [clinical safety assessment](/clinical-safety/process) is for.

## What to ask

<ul class="askq">
  <li>Does your product read from our clinical system, write to it, or both?</li>
  <li>What exactly does it write, and does a person check it first?</li>
  <li>What happens if the connection drops part-way through?</li>
  <li>Which of these risks are in your hazard log?</li>
</ul>
